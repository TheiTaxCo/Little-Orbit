Hello, grown-up.

Little Orbit is a gentle learning playground for ages 3–5. Sit together, hear the question, and let your child explore.

  • Count: tap each star to hear a number, then choose the total.
  • Colors & shapes: listen, look, and tap a match.
  • Memory: find every pair, then tap Next discovery for a fresh board.
  • Size Safari: find the big or small animal. Age 5 also explores medium.

Age 3 uses 1–3 stars, two answer choices, and two memory pairs. Age 4 uses 1–5 stars, three choices, and three pairs. Age 5 uses 1–10 stars, four choices, and four pairs. Size Safari compares two sizes at age 3 and three sizes at ages 4–5. Age 5 also asks for medium. Levels change on the next activity.

Use the speaker beside a question to hear it again. Voice availability and offline speech depend on your browser and device. If no voice is available, read prompts together.

Five rounds make one adventure. There are no timers, ads, accounts, or purchases. Stars and settings stay in this browser when local storage is available. No data is sent anywhere.
